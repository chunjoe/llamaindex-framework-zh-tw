`llama-index-llms-gemini` support has been discontinued as of version 0.6.2.

The package was replaced by `llama-index-llms-google-genai`. Visit the [Google GenAI page](https://docs.llamaindex.ai/en/stable/examples/llm/google_genai/) for the latest examples and documentation.
